What we offer

Every tool you need to
master your inventory

30-Day Sales Forecasting

ML models trained on your own historical data predict demand with upper and lower confidence bounds. Know what to order and when — before you run out or overstock.

Real-Time Risk Detection

Multi-factor scoring identifies overstock situations weeks before they become expensive. Get alerted on critical, high, and medium risk products automatically.

Smart Recommendations

AI-generated action items prioritized by urgency. Discount strategies, reorder timing, and clearance plans — fully automated and always up to date.

Simple CSV Upload

No technical setup required. Upload your products, sales history, and stock levels in 3 steps. Your personalized AI dashboard is live in under 5 minutes.
